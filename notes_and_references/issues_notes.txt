the type field is "manifold_base" a submodel or some other class has to be created to handle this other wise it isn't caught correctly

- solenoid_qty is determined from a specific valve_symbols field 'solenoid_qty'. For blanking plates, this is currently set to 0.
However, within component models for valves/blanking plates/ etc a manifold block is generated as double wired when a valve symbol with 
a solenoid_qty of 0 is selected --> blanking_plates. This could lead to an overload of max 32 pins (solenoids) in the case of say 31
single solenoid valves and 1 blanking plate (considered 2 solenoid) = 33.
----> potential fix would be re-allocating valve symbols to blanking plates such that they specify manifold block wiring
